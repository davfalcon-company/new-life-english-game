# New Life — A neighborhood to live in

Play: https://davfalcon-company.github.io/new-life-english-game/

A mobile English immersion game. The prologue is in Spanish; the world changes to English after arriving in the United States. Tap to walk and interact, and drag to explore.

Enter the market, diner, library, housing and your origin home. Walk inside, speak to the people, explore shelves and use objects. Work, earn promotions, cook, furnish a home, meet neighbors, and help with community jobs.

Your character’s phone offers a picture vocabulary guide, English/Spanish keyword search, pronunciation, example phrases, personal notes and saved conversation lines. Look things up without spending game time or losing an active conversation.

Progress stays in the browser on each device. Older saves migrate automatically. No account, backend or paid AI service is required. Conversations and vocabulary use authored content and local rules. This is a finite prototype, not a general translator or open-ended AI conversation system.

This repository contains published game assets only.
