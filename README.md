# New Life — Neighborhood Life

Play: https://davfalcon-company.github.io/new-life-english-game/

A mobile English immersion game. The prologue is in Spanish; everything changes to English after arriving in the United States. Tap to walk and interact, and drag to explore.

Build a life in two small neighborhoods: two careers with four roles each, promotion interviews and multi-task shifts; a home with six furnishings, cooking, studying and dinners; three neighbors with persistent memories and storylines; daily errands, news and English practice.

Progress stays on each device in the browser. Existing saves are migrated automatically. No account, backend or paid AI service is required. Conversations use authored content and local intent rules.

This repository contains the published game assets only.
