# New Life — Everyday Life

Play: https://davfalcon-company.github.io/new-life-english-game/

A mobile English immersion game. The prologue is in Spanish; everything changes to English after arriving in the United States. Tap to walk and interact, and drag to explore.

Build a life in two small neighborhoods: two careers with four roles each; a furnished home, cooking, studying and dinners; neighbors with memories and stories; daily errands, news and English practice.

The Everyday Life update adds place-themed mobile menus, a useful daily plan, a wallet with income/expense history and saving goals, eight paid odd jobs with saved progress, and four additional two-part conversation topics per neighbor.

Progress stays in the browser on each device. Older saves are migrated automatically. No account, backend or paid AI service is required. Conversations use authored content and local intent rules.

This repository contains the published game assets only.
